Read this section as description, not as a result. Finnish fungal recording grew from a few hundred records an autumn in the 1980s to over ten thousand, so an early year's rate is one or two finds divided by a denominator too small to divide by, and any variable that trends with time — temperature included — will correlate with the rate for that reason alone. The negative temperature correlation below is that artefact, not a finding. The matched sets in sections 1–3 are where the question is actually answered, because there the comparison is inside one place and one date.
